## Claims intentionally removed or prohibited

- Fictional testimonials, ratings, customer counts, broker/property counts, transactions, savings, 70%, 40%, or adoption claims.
- Waitlist totals, launch-soon language, generic `free`, and generic `trusted by brokers`.
- Universal `verified` or `100% verified` property/professional statements.
- Guaranteed 10-minute responses or guaranteed 24-hour transactions.
- AI Lakshmi, AI document verification, 3D/360 tours, Street View, built-in CRM, payment, invoice, SMS, push, digital-contract, and effortless deal-closure claims.
- Public search promotion while production search returns no useful inventory.
- Specific launch-market availability without owner, inventory, and coverage evidence.
- Testimonials, AggregateRating, reviews, awards, offers, and public pricing.
- Security certifications, penetration-test results, encryption-at-rest, breach-free, backup, or response-SLA claims without proof.
- Cloud-provider partnership, funding, approval, certification, or endorsement claims.

Product proof is limited to two sanitized, approved private-beta dashboard captures: the seeker property-search dashboard and the manager operations dashboard. Both use seeded test data, are dated 10 July 2026, contain no customer personal information, and are cropped only for clarity and equal presentation. Generated UI must never be presented as product evidence.
