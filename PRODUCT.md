# EstoSpaces Product Truth

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Public marketing website for a web-based property journey platform.

## Users

- Property seekers who want to discover relevant properties and understand what happens after an enquiry.
- Participating brokers and property managers who need clearer context for time-sensitive enquiries.
- Existing EstoSpaces account holders returning to the application.

## Product Purpose

EstoSpaces is software that connects property discovery, professional contact, response visibility, messages, documents, and next actions in one understandable journey.

## Positioning

The product is a private-beta property platform focused on making the next useful action clear. Participating professionals are measured against a 10-minute response target during supported operating periods. Fast Track is designed to help an enquiry progress toward a viewing or application within 24 hours.

## Operating Context

- Access and coverage are limited during private beta.
- Inventory and participating-professional availability vary by area.
- The public property-search service is not advertised as available until its production release checks pass.
- Registration and existing-user login remain available.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Registration and login links are real application routes.
- Search, saved-property, enquiry, messaging, document, and Fast Track experiences must not be shown as currently available if their production services have not passed release checks.
- The 10-minute response measure is a target, not a guarantee.
- The 24-hour Fast Track measure concerns progress toward a viewing or application, not completion of a tenancy or purchase.
- EstoSpaces does not claim estate-agency status.
- Property professionals remain responsible for their services and property information.
- Commercial terms and area coverage are confirmed directly, not promised on the public page.

## Brand Commitments

- Display name: EstoSpaces.
- Registered companies: Estospaces Solutions Private Limited in India and Estospaces Solutions Limited in the United Kingdom.
- Voice: direct, calm, transparent, and useful to technical and non-technical visitors.
- Preserve the existing EstoSpaces identity and registration/login destinations while replacing the generic visual treatment.

## Evidence on Hand

- Confirmed public-site content and current application routes.
- Existing privacy, security, terms, contact, accessibility, and cookie-control pages.
- Two sanitized private-beta dashboard captures are approved for the landing page: one seeker dashboard and one manager dashboard.
- Both captures use seeded test data, contain no customer personal information, and are labelled with their 10 July 2026 capture date.
- No approved testimonials, customer counts, property counts, or outcome statistics are available for public use.

## Product Principles

- Truth before conversion.
- Explain the next action, not just the feature.
- Show constraints close to the claim they qualify.
- Give seekers and property professionals equally clear paths.
- Prefer an authored narrative over a collection of generic feature cards.
- Keep the core page understandable without animation.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard operation.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with a complete static alternative.
- Do not delay navigation or core actions for animation.
- Keep registration, login, legal information, and support reachable without relying on JavaScript-driven motion.
